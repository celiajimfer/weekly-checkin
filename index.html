Before you start: how your information is handled

What we collect: a patient code (not your child's full name), your name, your email, your physiotherapist's email, and your answers about exercises, goals, mood and any concerns.

Why: so your check-in can be linked to the right file and, if you choose, sent to the correct physiotherapist.

Where it goes: nowhere, by default. Everything runs in your browser only. Nothing is uploaded, stored on a server, or seen by us. The only way anything leaves this page is if you press "Email your physiotherapist," which opens your own email app with the summary pre-filled — you decide whether to send it.

How long it's kept: your check-in answers exist only for this session and are cleared when you close the page. Your exercise and goal history is saved only on this device (browser local storage) under your patient code, so it can show your progress next time — it is never sent anywhere automatically and you can clear it any time by clearing your browser data.

Your rights: you can stop at any point by simply closing this page — nothing is submitted unless you actively send the email yourself. See the full privacy policy for more detail on data handling across the wider website.

Full data handling details

This tool stores exercise and goal check-marks locally on your device only, under the patient code you enter, so your progress shows up again next visit. This local data never leaves your device automatically. Emailing your results is entirely optional and initiated by you.
